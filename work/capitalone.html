01 — The problem

The hard part was never
the form.

Research showed people weren’t struggling with the fields. They were struggling with what the fields asked for: knowledge that lived in their heads, scattered across Slack, documents and offline conversations, plus standards they had to hunt down before they could check their own work.

What we heardThe knowledge is in people, not systems.

The system asked users to type out what they already knew, from scratch, every time.

What we heardThe context is everywhere.

Pieces of every record were spread across chat threads, docs and conversations nobody wrote down.

What we heardStandards are hard to find.

Before submitting, people had to dig up the right standard just to check their own work.

If the context lives in conversations, the interface should be one too.

02 — The strategy

Don’t click through risk.
Talk through it.

I lead the strategy for bringing chat into Risks, Controls and Issues & Events. You describe the situation in your own words. Fuse Assist, grounded in Capital One’s risk models, pulls the records, policies and test results, then drafts the record next to the conversation, so you never re-type what you already said.

03 — Designing for trust

Build confidence.
Keep people driving.

In a regulated bank, an AI answer that can’t be checked is an answer nobody can use. So every pattern does two jobs at once: it earns confidence in what the AI found, and it keeps the decision with the person who owns the risk.

04 — Designing with the model

The model keeps improving.
So does the interaction.

New models ship constantly, so I don’t design a fixed experience on top of one. I work alongside the AI modeling team and evolve the interaction as the model’s capabilities grow, giving the AI more of the gathering, drafting and checking as it earns it, while the person stays in charge of the decision.

AI findsSearch & summarize

Pull the right records, policies and test results.

AI draftsFill the record

Turn the conversation into a structured draft.

AI checksMatch the standard

Flag gaps against Capital One’s standards.

Always the personDecide & submit

Approval stays human, however capable the model gets.

06 — Closing the design–code gap

I stopped handing off.
I started shipping.

Instead of annotating the gap between Figma and code, I push my own PRs to the Fuse UI repo using TypeScript, Figma Code Connect, Figma MCP and Claude Code. I also re-engineered the Fuse Design System’s tokens so AI can generate and validate UI against them. Handoff is now 47% faster.
