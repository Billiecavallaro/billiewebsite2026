01 — The problem

Incidents ran 2.4× higher
than comparable tools.

A root cause study of incident tickets showed that most weren’t bugs at all. 94% were process or user errors: people couldn’t find the information they needed to do the task correctly.

49%
45%
6%
User errorProcess errorProduct malfunction

Those 94% became my use cases. I chose three KPIs to judge success against: incident volume, task completion rate and time on task.

02 — Strategy

Build or buy?

Before designing anything, I compared the cost and risk of each approach so the team could make the call with the full picture.

ServiceNow AI features

Benefits

  • Lower implementation cost
  • Faster time to value
  • Native to existing GRC workflows

Risks

  • Licensing and usage fees
  • Limited model customization
  • Vendor lock-in

In-house model

Benefits

  • Trained on internal GRC data and terminology
  • Full control over behavior and tuning
  • No per-use vendor fees

Risks

  • Higher upfront build cost
  • Ongoing maintenance and governance
  • Requires dedicated ML support

03 — Training the model

A model is only as good
as its data structure.

Align

Ran a workshop with the modeling, data science and PM teams to define what an AI-assisted interface needed to do.

Audit

Led a cross-team audit of every available input, then held weekly sessions to work through gaps and constraints.

Structure

Restructured scattered GRC knowledge into a clear hierarchy, giving the modelers a foundation they could train on with confidence.
